# Healthcare AI Tools

General-purpose AI tooling for healthcare workflows.

## Areas

- **Cardiology** - Cardiac monitoring and risk signals.
- **Oncology** - Tumor data and treatment workflows.
- **Neurology** - Neurological assessment support.
- **Endocrinology** - Hormonal and metabolic data.
- **General** - Vitals, triage, and records.

## Notes

- Provider-agnostic: the tools work with any compatible backend.
- Findings are decision support, not a substitute for a professional.
- Sensitive data must be handled according to local regulations.
